# Volume Theory: Black Hole Event Horizons as Spatial Grid Coordinate Overflow

A conceptual hypothesis attempting to bridge general relativity, the discrete nature of space, and an information-centric view of black hole collapse.

---

## Overview

**Volume Theory** is a thought experiment and algorithmic framework designed to model space not as a continuous smooth manifold, but as a discrete coordinate grid with finite capacity limits. 

Under extreme gravitational conditions, traditional physics encounters mathematical singularities (divide-by-zero errors). Volume Theory replaces these infinities with a limit state caused by **informational and coordinate overflow** within the spatial matrix.

---

## Key Postulates & Core Logic

### 1. Gravitational Field Intensity Scale (beta)
Space curvature and gravitational potential are measured via a dimensionless scalar parameter **beta**, bounded between 0 and 1:

* **beta = 0:** Absolute vacuum. Standard flow of time, complete absence of mass and distortion.
* **0 < beta < 1:** Standard regions of mass (planets, main-sequence stars, white dwarfs). Time dilation and space bending obey standard General Relativity laws.
* **beta → 1:** The event horizon limit. Time for an outside observer asymptotically stops, and space grid distortion reaches its absolute capacity limit.

---

### 2. Post-Iron Phase Transitions

When a massive star exhausts its nuclear fuel and undergoes catastrophic gravitational collapse, matter transitions through distinct phase states:

#### A. Hyper-ms (Quantum Coordinate Overlap)
Upon reaching extreme density, standard atomic and subatomic structures break down. On a discrete spatial grid, particles lose the ability to occupy unique coordinates. An **"overlap" effect** occurs: multiple elements shift into identical coordinate cells, forming a fundamental substance of extreme density — **Hyper-m**.

#### B. Densirite Matter
A massive concentration of Hyper-ms forms a macrostructure called **Densirite**.
* Each individual Hyper-m unit carries a local gravitational coefficient where **beta ≈ 0.999999...**
* Densirite acts as a unified high-density array whose cumulative mass bends the quantum spatial grid inward upon itself.

---

### 3. Coordinate Overflow & Event Horizon Buffer

The central thesis of Volume Theory is that a black hole is a state of **spatial coordinate overflow**:

When a mass of Densirite compresses to the point where the local coefficient reaches the absolute limit ($1 - \epsilon$), the spatial grid loses the capacity to compute or render standard physical interactions within normal coordinates.

Rather than collapsing into an infinite singularity, the spacetime grid snaps inward and closes on itself. The event horizon acts as a **hard buffer limit**, storing mass and information in accordance with holographic principles.
